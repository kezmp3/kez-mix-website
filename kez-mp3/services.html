Turnaround Time

Current turnaround is typically 7–14 days. During peak season, please allow longer as the schedule fills quickly. To guarantee availability and meet your performance timeline, book your mix as early as possible.

Rush delivery is offered when availability permits — reach out for a quote.

FAQ

Good to know before booking

Do I need to supply the songs?

Yes — bring the song list and any timestamps you want included. Cover-track requirements can be discussed if your event requires licensed audio.

Can I request revisions?

All packages include 3 revisions. Any additional revisions after that are $10 each.

What if I need it faster?

Rush delivery is available depending on current booking load — mention your deadline on the form.

How do I pay?

Payment details are confirmed after your quote is finalized, before production begins.
